Our story

From Autokton Agency to Brandt Core.

We started in 2021 as Autokton Agency — designing brands, running social media and building websites for businesses in Kosovo and abroad.

Along the way we started building our own software. Brandt Bot went from an idea to a product with paying customers. Projekto and Ustahu followed. We learned what it really takes to design, build and sell a product.

Then AI changed the rules. Much of what agencies used to sell can now be done in minutes. What still matters is knowing what to build, connecting it to how a business really works, and making it look and feel right.

So we rebuilt the company around that. Brandt Core brings creative work, engineering and AI under one roof — for businesses that want to grow, not just look busy.
