(06) Questions

Questions we hear most.

Have another one? Write to us.

How much does a project cost?

Every project gets a fixed price after the first call. You know the full cost before anything starts.

How long does it take?

A website takes two to four weeks, a platform or app six to twelve. The deadline is written into the proposal.

Do you work with clients outside Kosovo?

Yes. We work in English, German and Albanian, with clients across Europe.

Do I need to know anything technical about AI?

No. You tell us where time is lost; we build it, connect it and train your team.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Code, design and data are yours once the project is paid in full.
